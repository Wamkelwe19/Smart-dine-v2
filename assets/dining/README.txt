Dining hall photos bundled with the app.

Name each photo after the cafeteria, lowercase, with spaces and punctuation
replaced by hyphens:

  "Main Dining Hall"   ->  main-dining-hall.jpg
  "Mbombela Cafe"      ->  mbombela-cafe.png

Supported: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp. Landscape (16:9), about 1600px wide, under
500 KB each keeps the app small.

A photo uploaded by staff in the portal (Settings -> Cafeteria photo) always
takes priority over the bundled one.
